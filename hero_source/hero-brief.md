## Headline figure to composite (exact text)

Main figure: "53.1 vs 64.8"
Sub-line: "Median site EUI (kBtu per square foot): the 984 buildings reporting in both 2016 and 2025 report 53.1 in 2025 against 64.8 in 2016"
Source line: "Machine Readable - Energize Denver Final Master Datasets 2016 and 2025, pulled Oct 29, 2026 - matched panel of 984 buildings"

## Scene description (story-driven, not decorative)

A dusk Denver skyline of large buildings - downtown towers, a school,
a multifamily block, a hospital - seen from a slightly elevated
angle, each building carrying a heat glow in amber and teal that
reads as energy intensity: a few buildings glow bright amber, most
sit in cooler teal tones, and the glow across the skyline visibly
dims from the left (2016) toward the right (2025) without going dark.
The visual argument is the brief's: the same large-building stock,
using measurably less energy per square foot than it did when
benchmarking began. Keep the scene generic - no real building names,
no readable signage, no text baked into the art itself.

## Composition requirements

- Brand palette: dark charcoal base, amber and teal accents.
- Leave a clean, dark, low-detail field across the top third (or a
  gradient scrim over it) for the headline figure; headline text must
  be fully legible, never washed out over busy art.
- Composite the headline figure and source line from the exact text
  above; do not paraphrase the numbers.
- Both numbers carry the same measure and scope on the graphic
  itself (median site EUI, matched panel of 984 buildings), per the
  framing standard.
