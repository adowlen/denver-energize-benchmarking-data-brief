## LinkedIn draft (company page, for a future Friday synthesis)

53.1 vs 64.8.

That is the median site EUI, in kBtu per square foot, for the same
984 Denver buildings reporting in both 2016 and 2025: 64.8 then,
53.1 now. 73.7% of them report a lower intensity in 2025.

On the program's own baseline year the panel is bigger and the
move is the same: 1,687 buildings, median 65.9 in 2019 to 52.4 in
2025. The highest median of any large building type in 2025 is
supermarkets at 157.8; the lowest is self-storage at 9.7.

Two limits ride with every number here: site EUI is as reported
through Portfolio Manager, not metered by the city, and intensity
per square foot cannot separate emptier buildings from more
efficient ones. The brief prints both panels, the weather-
normalized check, and the strongest alternative explanation,
with the data.

Full brief, data, and methods:
